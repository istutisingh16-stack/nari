Online nutritionist for women

Make room for nutrition advice that fits your food preferences, routine and budget. Speak with a NARI nutritionist by video about everyday eating, weight goals or nutrition questions alongside your existing women’s health care.

Nutrition support built around your routine

Bring the questions that make eating well feel complicated: planning meals around work, choosing vegetarian options, managing an irregular schedule or making sense of conflicting diet advice. Explain what you normally eat and what you would like help with, so the consultation starts with your own circumstances.

PCOS, pregnancy and changing life stages

You can ask about nutrition alongside PCOS care, pregnancy, the months after childbirth or menopause. Tell the care team your life stage when booking so they can confirm the appropriate expert. Nutrition support complements medical care; it does not replace assessment by your treating clinician.

Prepare for your nutrition consultation

Have a note of your usual meals, food preferences, allergies, current medicines or supplements, and any reports you already have. You do not need to buy tests or supplements to make an enquiry. Bring one or two practical goals you want to discuss, and ask about follow-up support before choosing a plan.

Booking and consultation fees

A single video consultation is ₹199. For ongoing care, NARI also offers ₹799 for 7 days or ₹2,499 for 30 days. Ask the care team to confirm the expert, available slot and what your chosen plan includes. Clinic visits are quoted separately.
