One team for every part of a woman's health

Women's health is rarely one problem. Irregular periods come with weight change and anxiety; a difficult delivery leaves back pain and low mood; menopause touches sleep, joints and confidence. Yet care is split across clinics that never talk to each other. NARI puts a gynaecologist, a female physiotherapist, a psychologist and a nutritionist on the same panel, so your care fits together.

Every expert is licence-verified. You book in under a minute, consult privately by video from anywhere in India, and choose a partner clinic visit in Bulandshahr, Noida or Delhi NCR when you want to be seen in person. If you are not sure who to see, tell us what is going on and we match you.
