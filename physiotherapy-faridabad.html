Discuss pain, mobility and your daily tasks

Tell the physiotherapist about desk work, lifting, walking or pregnancy-related movement concerns. Bring relevant medical advice and explain what makes symptoms better or worse. Ask how a rehabilitation plan would be assessed and reviewed, and whether medical evaluation is needed first.

Confirm Faridabad appointment availability

Give the coordinator your sector or locality in Faridabad and whether you want video, clinic or home-visit availability checked. Confirm the exact address, professional, fee and appointment time before travelling. If no suitable local slot is available, ask whether a video consultation is appropriate for your concern.

NARI coordinates care through its panel and partner arrangements. This page does not represent a walk-in NARI clinic at a published address. Local visits depend on the right professional being available.

Consultation fees and preparation

A single video consultation is ₹199. Ongoing plans are ₹799 for 7 days or ₹2,499 for 30 days. Ask what your selected plan includes; clinic and home-visit charges are quoted separately.

Have your questions, current medicines and relevant reports ready. Tell the expert about ongoing treatment and any restrictions from your clinician. A video appointment may need to be followed by an in-person assessment.
