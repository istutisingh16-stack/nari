## 1. Google Search Console (Most Important — Do This First)

This is how you tell Google your website exists and make sure it gets indexed.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Click **"Start now"** and sign in with your Google account
3. Click **"Add a property"** → choose **"URL prefix"** → type: `https://narihealth.in`
4. Google will give you a verification file (an HTML file). Download it.
5. Upload that file to the root of your website (same folder as index.html on GitHub)
6. Come back to Search Console and click **Verify**
7. Once verified, go to **Sitemaps** in the left menu → type `sitemap.xml` → click **Submit**

**Why this matters:** Without this, Google is only guessing your site exists. Submitting the sitemap tells Google exactly which pages to index and gets you in results faster.

---

## 2. Google Business Profile (Free — Puts You on Maps)

This is how NARI shows up when someone searches "women's health platform" or "physiotherapy for women" on Google Maps.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Click **"Manage now"** → Sign in with your Google account
3. Search for "NARI Health" — if it's not there, click **"Add your business"**
4. Fill in:
   - **Business name:** NARI Health
   - **Category:** Women's Health Clinic *(or: Physical Therapist)*
   - **Business type:** Service area business *(you don't need a physical address)*
   - **Service area:** India *(or specific cities you serve)*
   - **Phone:** your number
   - **Website:** https://narihealth.in
   - **Hours:** As on your website
5. Verify via phone/postcard (Google will guide you)
6. Once live, add your logo and some photos

**Why this matters:** Local/Map results appear above regular search results. This is often how people find health services. It's completely free.

## 5. Google Analytics 4 (Track Your Visitors)

This shows you how many people visit your website, where they come from, and which pages they view.

**Steps:**
1. Go to [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com)
2. Click **"Start measuring"** → create an account for NARI Health
3. Create a property → select **Web** → enter `narihealth.in`
4. You'll get a measurement ID (looks like `G-XXXXXXXXXX`)
5. Send that ID to your developer — they'll add it to your website in 5 minutes

**Why this matters:** Without analytics, you're flying blind. You won't know what's working.
