Online gynaecology consultations

Talk about period concerns, PCOS, pregnancy questions or menopause with a gynaecologist through NARI Health. Start with a booking enquiry and the care team will confirm availability and the consultation format.

A place to discuss your concerns

You can bring questions about irregular or painful periods, changes in your cycle, an existing PCOS diagnosis, pregnancy care or menopause. Describe the concern in your own words; you do not need to decide on a diagnosis before asking for an appointment.

Video or an in-person appointment

A video appointment gives you space to discuss your history and questions. If you need an examination, tests or in-person care, discuss the next step with the clinician. Ask the care team about partner-clinic availability in Bulandshahr, Noida or Delhi NCR before making travel plans. Video consultations are not an emergency service.

What to have ready

Bring your main questions, relevant previous reports, a list of current medicines and, if useful, a record of your period dates. If you already have a treating doctor, explain your current care plan. You can also ask the coordinator about nutrition or emotional wellbeing support alongside your consultation.

Booking and consultation fees

A single video consultation is ₹199. For ongoing care, NARI also offers ₹799 for 7 days or ₹2,499 for 30 days. Ask the care team to confirm the expert, available slot and what your chosen plan includes. Clinic visits are quoted separately.
