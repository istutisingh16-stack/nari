Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy matters deeply to us. NARI Health is committed to protecting the personal and health information you share with us. This policy explains clearly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Who We Are

NARI Health is a women led holistic health platform offering physiotherapy, psychology and nutrition consultations online and via home visits across India. You can reach us at narihealthh@gmail.com or on Instagram at @_nari.health.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Health information you share when describing your condition or goals
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Messages and communication you send through our contact form or WhatsApp

We do not collect payment information directly — any payments are processed through third-party providers under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and book consultations
  • Match you with the right specialist for your needs
  • Send appointment reminders and follow-up care information
  • Improve our services based on feedback

We will never sell your information to third parties or use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Health Information

Health information is treated with the highest level of confidentiality. Information shared during consultations is:

  • Accessible only to the specialist assigned to your care
  • Never shared outside NARI without your explicit consent, except where required by law
  • Stored securely and handled in accordance with applicable Indian data protection regulations

5. How We Share Information

We use the following third-party tools to operate our services:

  • EmailJS — to receive contact form submissions securely
  • WhatsApp — for direct communication; subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome — for website typography and icons

Each of these providers has their own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at narihealthh@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your contact and health information for as long as you remain a NARI client, or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we encourage you to contact us directly if you have concerns.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will always be available on this page with a revised date at the top. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:

  • Email: narihealthh@gmail.com
  • Instagram: @_nari.health
  • Website: narihealth.in
