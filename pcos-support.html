Searching for a PCOS cure? Here is what care can offer.

PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) currently has no cure. Treatment can help manage symptoms and support long-term health. The approach depends on your concerns and goals, including periods, skin changes or fertility. The World Health Organization’s PCOS fact sheet explains the condition and available approaches. NARI does not promise a permanent cure or a fixed recovery time.

PCOS consultation questions

Can I get PCOS treatment online?

You can discuss PCOS concerns and an existing care plan with a gynaecologist by video. The clinician decides whether an examination, tests or an in-person appointment are needed. An online booking does not guarantee a diagnosis or prescription.

Can nutrition alone cure PCOS?

No. PCOS has no established cure. Healthy eating and physical activity are part of care, even without weight loss, according to WHO. A nutrition consultation can support food planning alongside medical care; it is not a replacement for the clinician’s assessment.

Do irregular periods mean I have PCOS?

Not necessarily. Irregular periods can occur with PCOS, but other causes need to be considered. A clinician assesses your history and decides what further evaluation is appropriate.

Can I ask about fertility?

Yes, you can bring fertility questions to a gynaecology consultation. Ask what evaluation or referral may be needed. NARI does not promise pregnancy outcomes or advertise fertility procedures as part of this consultation service.

Do I need to buy a membership?

No. You can start with a single ₹199 video consultation. Ask about ongoing plans only if you want follow-up care. In-person fees and availability are confirmed separately.
